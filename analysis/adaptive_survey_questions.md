# Questions for adaptive_survey (findings1.csv)

The following questions were used in the adaptive version of the Campus Social Experience survey. Questions were branched based on user responses.

## Section A — Screening
- **Q1**: Do you generally like interacting with new people on campus?
  - (Options: Yes, Sometimes, Not really, Prefer known people)

## Section B — Recent Real Interaction
- **Q2**: Can you tell us about the last time you talked to someone new on campus? (Open text)
- **Q2A**: Where did this happen? (In class, Group assignment, Club, Event, etc.)
- **Q2B**: What started the conversation? (Shared task, Academic need, Shared interest, They approached, I approached, etc.)
- **Q2C**: Who initiated the conversation? (I did, They did, Natural/Both, Don't remember)
- **Q2D**: How comfortable did that interaction feel? (Scale 1-5)
- **Q2E**: (If uncomfortable) What made that interaction uncomfortable? (Felt awkward, No common interests, Too intense, etc.)
- **Q2F**: (If comfortable) What made that interaction feel comfortable or easy? (Flowed naturally, Friendly person, Common topic, etc.)

## Section C — Contexts and Natural Interaction
- **Q4**: Are there situations where talking to new people feels easier or more natural for you? (Yes, Sometimes, No)
- **Q4A**: Which situations feel easier? (Shared activities, Small groups, One-on-one, Informal, Someone else starts, etc.)
- **Q4B**: Why do these situations feel easier? (Shared purpose, Natural start, Less pressure, Common ground, etc.)

## Section D — Barriers and Motivation
- **Q5**: Are there situations where you usually avoid starting a conversation with someone new? (Yes, Sometimes, No)
- **Q5A**: What makes those situations difficult? (People in groups, Don't know how to start, Judgment concern, No reason to talk, etc.)
- **Q5B**: What do you usually do instead? (Wait for someone, Observe, Talk only if needed, Stick to known people, etc.)
- **Q6**: What usually motivates you to talk to someone you do not know yet? (Shared interest, Academic reason, Mutual friend, Curiosity, Networking, etc.)

## Section E — Behaviour Patterns
- **Q7**: When you join a new group or event, how do you usually behave? (Start quickly, Clear reason, Wait for someone, Observe first, Avoid)
- **Q7A**: (If start quickly) What makes you comfortable initiating? (Experience, Natural, Approachable)
- **Q7B**: (If clear reason) What kind of reason usually helps? (Task help, Shared obs, Mutual friend)
- **Q7C**: (If wait) What makes it easier when the other person starts first? (No ice-breaking pressure, Interest signal, Response preference)
- **Q7D**: (If observe) Before starting, what do you look for? (Approachable, Common interest, Openness, Natural situation)
- **Q7E**: (If avoid) What usually stops you from engaging? (Groups, Fear of rejection, Timing, Energy)
- **Q8**: Do you find it easier to talk to someone one-on-one or as part of a group activity? (One-on-one, Group, Depends, Neither)
- **Q8A**: (If one-on-one) Why? (Depth, Less noise, Lower pressure)
- **Q8B**: (If group) Why? (Shared task, Less initiation pressure, Organic flow)
- **Q8C**: (If depends) What makes the situation matter? (Mood, Size, Known people)
- **Q8D**: (If neither) What would make interaction feel easier for you? (Anxiety tools, Structure, Online-to-offline bridge, Interest matching)

## Section F — Digital Interaction Relevance
- **Q9**: Do you think it is easier to interact with new people online or in person? (Online, In person, Similar, Neither)
- **Q9A**: (If online) Why? (Less pressure, More time, Less fear of judgment, Easier approach)
- **Q9B**: (If in person) Why? (Natural flow, Reading the person, Connection depth)
- **Q9C**: (If similar) In what situations does each work better? (Social vs. Task-based)
- **Q9D**: (If neither) Why are both hard? (Fundamental difficulty, Initiation friction, Matching interests)

## Section Wrap-up
- **Q10**: If you could improve how students meet and connect with new people on campus, what would make that easier? (Open text)
