# Research Overview: Understanding the Student Social Initiation Gap

This document summarizes the core research areas for the Campus Social Experience study, focusing on the barriers to entry for spontaneous student connections.

## 1. Primary Research Question
How do psychological and situational barriers impact a student's ability to initiate a conversation with someone they do not know on campus?

## 2. Key Objectives
- **Mapping Barriers**: Identifying the weight of individual psychological (anxiety, overthinking) vs. situational (lack of reason, group formation) barriers.
- **Contextual Reliance**: Understanding if students strictly require a shared context (like a class or club) to interact.
- **Digital Bridging**: Evaluating the potential for digital "first steps" to lower the barrier for offline interaction.

## 3. High-Potential Markers
Behavioral markers derived from survey data that indicate strong alignment with SparkCues' value proposition:
- **Initiation Gap**: High desire for connection coupled with low initiation confidence.
- **Context Dependence**: Reliance on structural cues before engaging.
- **Anxiety Clusteredness**: Overlapping concerns of judgment and overthinking.
