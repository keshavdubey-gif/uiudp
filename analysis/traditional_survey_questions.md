# Questions and Headers for traditional_survey (findings2.csv)

The following headers and descriptions explain the data points captured in the previous, non-adaptive version of the Campus Social Experience survey.

## 1. Demographics
- **age_group**: (18-20, 21-22, 23-24, 25+)
- **year_of_study**: (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th+)
- **program**: (B.Tech, M.Tech, Dual Degree, MBA, etc.)
- **gender**: (Male, Female, Non-binary, etc.)
- **residence**: (Hostel, Day Scholar, Off-campus)

## 2. PANAS (Positive and Negative Affect Schedule)
Respondents rated how much they felt the following on a scale of 1-5 (Very Slightly/Not at all to Extremely):
- **panas_1**: Interested
- **panas_2**: Distressed
- **panas_3**: Excited
- **panas_4**: Upset
- **panas_5**: Strong
- **panas_6**: Guilty
- **panas_7**: Scared
- **panas_8**: Hostile
- **panas_9**: Enthusiastic
- **panas_10**: Proud
- **panas_11**: Irritable
- **panas_12**: Alert
- **panas_13**: Ashamed
- **panas_14**: Inspired
- **panas_15**: Nervous
- **panas_16**: Determined
- **panas_17**: Attentive
- **panas_18**: Jittery
- **panas_19**: Active
- **panas_20**: Afraid

## 3. Social Interaction Contexts
- **social_satisfaction**: "How satisfied are you with your social life on campus?"
- **belonging**: "How much do you feel you belong at this university?"
- **close_friends**: "How many close friends do you have on campus?"
- **social_isolation**: "How often do you feel left out or isolated?"
- **social_frequency**: "How often do you interact with new people on campus?"

## 4. Interaction Mechanisms
- **friendship_ease**: "How easy is it for you to make new friends?"
- **initiation_anxiety**: "How much anxiety do you feel when initiating a conversation?"
- **overthinking**: "How much do you overthink social interactions?"
- **avoidance**: "How often do you avoid social situations?"
- **judgment_concern**: "How much do you worry about being judged by others?"
- **conversation_initiator**: "In a new interaction, who usually starts the conversation?"
- **first_interaction_comfort**: "How comfortable do you feel during the first few minutes of a new interaction?"

## 5. Friendship Sources (Booleans — select all that apply)
- **fs_classes**: Friends made in classes
- **fs_hostel**: Friends made in the hostel
- **fs_clubs**: Friends made in clubs or societies
- **fs_mutual**: Friends made through mutual friends
- **fs_online**: Friends made online (campus groups, etc.)

## 6. Interaction Barriers (Booleans — select all that apply)
- **ib_fear_rejection**: Fear of rejection
- **ib_not_knowing**: Not knowing how to start
- **ib_formed_groups**: People already in formed groups
- **ib_no_topic**: No common topic or reason to talk
- **ib_low_energy**: Low social energy
- **ib_nothing**: No barriers (found it easy)

## 7. Psychological Trait Scoring (Calculated)
- **trait_er**: Emotional Regulation (composure in social uncertainty)
- **trait_cr**: Cognitive Reflection (depth of processing before acting)
- **trait_si**: Social Initiative (likelihood of starting connections)
- **trait_pf**: Psychological Flexibility (comfort outside your usual circle)
- **trait_gp**: Goal Persistence (commitment to meaningful relationships)
- **trait_se**: Self-Insight (awareness of your own social patterns)
- **trait_ed**: Exploratory Drive (desire for new social experiences)
- **archetype**: The assigned behavioral persona (e.g., Catalyst, Inner Architect, Bridge Builder)

## 8. Open-Ended Questions
- **social_friction_open**: "What makes it harder for you to talk to someone new?"
- **safety_factors**: "What would make social interaction feel safer or more comfortable?"
